Measure one foot

Use one plain A4 sheet in portrait (vertical) orientation. Place one bare foot on it; measure the larger foot if one is noticeably bigger.

  1. Position the phone camera directly above the sheet, not at an angle.
  2. Make sure the whole sheet and your foot are visible in good light.
  3. After capturing, tap the two top paper corners, your heel, longest toe and both sides at the widest part of your foot.

This is an approximate home measurement. Product sizing varies by brand and style, so please confirm the size with our shop team.
