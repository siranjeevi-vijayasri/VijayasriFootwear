Vijayasri foot-mask annotator

Offline annotation helper for the independent foot segmentation model. Images stay in this browser tab and are not uploaded.

Click around the outside edge of one bare foot. Include every toe and the heel. Stop at the ankle crease; do not include the lower leg, paper or floor. Click at least 12 points for a useful outline, then choose Finish outline.

Choose a local image to begin.

1. Select image2. Click foot boundary3. Finish outline4. Export matching PNG mask
Dataset reminder

Use consented images only. Save source images and exported masks into the same split folder (train, val or test), using identical filename stems. Keep every person's images in only one split. See DATASET.md for the annotation boundary and privacy rules.
